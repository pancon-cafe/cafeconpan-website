Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Loops is an iPhone app made by Café Con Pan LLC ("Café Con Pan," "we," or "us"). This policy explains what information Loops collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It covers the Loops app and the services that run it. Our website has its own privacy policy.

At a glance

  • We never sell your data, show you ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • Your contacts, calendar, and photo library stay on your iPhone.
  • Location is only used by features that need it, and Nearby Alerts is off unless you turn it on.
  • During Close Loop, your name is only shared with a phone that's within arm's reach.
  • You can delete your account and your data at any time, right in the app.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, password, username, display name, and date of birth. We use your date of birth to confirm you're 18 or older and to show only offers you're old enough for, such as 21+ offers. Your password is stored in hashed form; we can't see it.
  • Profile (optional): a profile photo, bio, city, Instagram and TikTok usernames, and a profile song (its title, artist, artwork, and Apple Music link).
  • Your loops: the people you connect with, the relationship type you choose, how often you want to meet up, and any note you add to a loop request.
  • Messages you send to people in your loops.
  • Memories: photos and videos you choose to save, who you tag, and where they were taken if you allow location access.
  • City Loop: check-ins at local spots, and the ratings, reviews, photos, and likes you post.
  • Safety and support: reports you file, people you block, and feedback you send us.

Information collected as you use Loops

  • Activity: hangouts and check-ins (who and when), streaks, milestones, and reactions.
  • Profile views: when someone opens your profile, we keep a record of the view for a day or two so each person is counted once, then keep only the total.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, your device's push token, so we can deliver them. It's removed when you sign out.
  • Location while you use the app: to show how far away City Loop spots are and to tag where a memory was taken. Distances are worked out on your iPhone. Your location is only sent to us as part of a memory you save.
  • Nearby Alerts (off unless you turn it on): Loops checks your location in the background when you've moved a significant distance and stores your most recent location on our servers. We use it only to let you and someone in your loop know when you're both within about 300 meters (1,000 feet), and only if you've both turned the feature on. Turning it off deletes your stored location.
  • Close Loop: uses Ultra Wideband and a direct connection between the two iPhones. The distance between the phones is measured on the phones themselves and is never sent to us. Until the phones are within about 1.5 meters (5 feet), they share only a temporary code; your name and account ID are shared after that, and only with that phone. When you both confirm, we record the check-in.

On your device

What stays on your iPhone

  • Contacts are shown only so you can text a friend an invite. We never upload them.
  • Calendar: Loops can add events you choose to create. It can't read your calendar.
  • Photo library: Loops can save the photos and videos you capture. When you pick an existing photo, Apple's picker shares only the one you choose.
  • Widgets and Live Activities show your loops and streaks using data stored on your iPhone.

How we use it

How we use information

  • To run Loops: your account, loops, messages, check-ins, memories, City Loop, and notifications.
  • To keep people safe: we review reports within 24 hours, remove content that breaks our Terms of Use, and suspend accounts that abuse others.
  • To improve Loops: we look at how features are used, like how many check-ins happen at a City Loop spot.
  • To share totals with local businesses: partner businesses may see numbers such as how many Loops visits they had or their average rating. These never identify you.
  • To talk with you: to answer support requests and send important notices about your account.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Visibility

What other people can see

  • Any Loops user can find your profile (username, display name, photo, bio, city, profile song, and social usernames) by searching.
  • People in your loops also see your hangouts and streaks with them, the memories you share with them, and your messages to them.
  • Activity like new loops, closed loops, and memories you save shows up in the Pulse feed for people in your loops. A new loop can also appear to people in the other person's loops.
  • City Loop ratings, reviews, and photos can be seen by other Loops users.

Sharing

Who we share information with

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. We share it only with the companies that help us run Loops:

SupabaseDatabase, sign-in, file storage, and server functions. Your data is stored in the United States.
ApplePush notifications, maps and place search, and Apple Music song search.
YelpRatings for City Loop spots. Our server sends Yelp only the business's name and location, never anything about you.
Google WorkspaceOur support email.

We may also share information when the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a merger or sale of our business. If that would change how your information is handled, we'll tell you first.

Retention

How long we keep information

  • We keep your information while your account is open.
  • Deleting your account (Profile → Settings → Delete Account) permanently removes your account, profile, loops, messages, memories, photos, check-ins, reviews, and reactions. Conversations you had are removed for the other person too.
  • A few records are kept without your name: reports you filed (so we can finish reviewing them), feedback you sent, City Loop visits you made with someone else, and memories a friend saved that you were tagged in.
  • Deleting a conversation hides it for you. The other person keeps their copy.
  • Deleted data may remain in backups for a short time before it's overwritten.

Your choices

Your choices and rights

  • Change what Loops can access (location, notifications, camera, contacts, and more) anytime in your iPhone's Settings app.
  • Turn Nearby Alerts on or off in Loops under Profile → Settings.
  • Block anyone, and report profiles, messages, or photos, from inside the app.
  • To see, correct, or get a copy of your data, or to delete it, email support@pancon.cafe with the subject "Privacy Request." We'll respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example, Virginia or California), state privacy laws may give you additional rights. We honor them for everyone. If we turn down a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer. We don't sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising, so there's nothing you need to opt out of.

Age

Adults only

Loops is only for people 18 and older, and we check date of birth when you sign up. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think someone under 18 is using Loops, report their profile in the app or email support@pancon.cafe, and we'll delete the account.

Security

Keeping your information safe

We encrypt data as it travels between your iPhone and our servers, limit access so each person can only reach their own information, and never store passwords in plain text. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password you don't use anywhere else.

Updates

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll let you know in the app before they take effect.
